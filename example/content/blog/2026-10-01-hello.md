This site uses the sample theme. The theme ships two files: `partials/footer.php` and `assets/style.css`. Every other template, partial and translation comes from the built-in `minimal` theme.

> [!TIP]
> Start your own theme by copying `package/` and editing the bottom part of `assets/style.css`.
